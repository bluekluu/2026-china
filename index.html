Before you go

Planning and booking priorities

Advance reservations

Nanjing Museum and main Suzhou Museum are timed/real-name visits; check garden entry rules, Gulangyu ferry tickets, and your original ID requirements.

Transport windows

Use official 12306 for mainland rail. Many tickets are released about 15 days out; a special earlier request pilot exists on certain Beijing–Shanghai routes, but does not guarantee these services.

Zhuhai: no border crossing

No Macau day trip, ferry or border excursion is included. The Hengqin riverfront outing stays inside mainland China; use DiDi between Zhuhai sights.

Sources and caveats

Official hotels, museum sites and public transport sources were prioritized. Restaurant recommendations are branch-sensitive; check current listings and hours on Amap or Dianping on the day.
